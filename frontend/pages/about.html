WiseLanka helps Sri Lankan learners explore education and career pathways based on their qualifications, interests and future goals.

Whether you are finishing school, building practical skills or continuing your studies, WiseLanka brings pathway information and clear explanations together so you can make a more informed decision.

Our purpose

Make the next decision clearer.

Choosing a course or career means understanding the entry requirements, how your qualifications fit, and where a course might lead.

WiseLanka connects these pieces. It helps you explore options, understand the reasons behind a recommendation and consider alternative routes when your first choice needs more preparation.

The aim is to support your decision with information you can understand, check and discuss with your family, teachers or an institution.

Understanding your results

Know why an option appears.

WiseLanka uses curated education information and explicit academic rules. It explains the available evidence so you can understand the result.

Optional interest-based ranking can organise options by relevance. It does not change academic requirements or turn an unmet condition into a satisfied one.

Requirements met
The recorded requirements are satisfied by the evidence you supplied. This is not an offer of admission.
Not assessed
More information or a provider's review is needed. Missing evidence does not automatically mean you are unsuitable.
Requirements not met
At least one assessed condition is unmet. Read the explanation and explore other routes or further preparation.

About the project

Guidance built around clear reasoning.

WiseLanka is a University of Westminster final-year academic software project focused on explainable education and career pathway guidance for Sri Lankan learners.

The prototype brings programme, qualification, entry requirement, progression, career, skills and scholarship records together. Development includes automated testing and source-reference checks.

Its catalogue is curated and limited, and further learner evaluation and manual validation remain areas for development. WiseLanka is a guidance tool; institutions and providers make their own admissions and funding decisions.

Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before you begin.

Does WiseLanka guarantee admission or a job?

No. Recommendations help you explore options using the available evidence. A matching score is not a probability of admission, and a career connection is not an employment promise. Confirm current requirements directly with the provider.

What if my results are incomplete?

Provide the information you have and indicate what is missing. Some requirements may remain unassessed until more evidence is supplied. WiseLanka does not automatically verify awards or assume equivalence between qualifications.

Can I use Sinhala or Tamil?

The learner profile offers English, Sinhala and Tamil options. Sinhala and Tamil translations are drafts and still require fluent review. Language support may vary by feature and device.

How does the optional relevance ranking work?

The prototype can use a frozen pretrained language model to compare your stated interests and career intentions with programme descriptions. It orders options within academic status groups while keeping eligibility rules in control. If the model is unavailable, the system uses a rule-based fallback.

Does that model send my profile to an external AI provider?

The documented optional relevance model runs on the application server using downloaded model files. It does not send learner profiles to an external AI provider for that ranking step. This describes that feature rather than a complete privacy policy for every deployment.
