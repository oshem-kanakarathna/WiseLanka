FAQs

Got questions?
Start here.

Clear answers before you take your next step.

What is WiseLanka?

An academic software project that helps Sri Lankan learners explore education and career pathways through curated information and explainable rules.

Where should I begin?

Choose your education stage or build your learner profile. The guided form takes you through education, interests, goals and a final review.

Does a match guarantee admission?

No. Matching information is guidance based on the evidence available. Confirm current admission and selection requirements directly with the institution.

What if my results are incomplete?

Indicate which results are not reported. Requirements may remain unassessed until sufficient evidence is available.

Can I explore another route?

WiseLanka includes recorded qualification progression links and alternative routes. Each route may have conditions that need provider confirmation.

Can I use Sinhala or Tamil?

The learner profile offers English, Sinhala and Tamil. Sinhala and Tamil translations are drafts awaiting fluent review.
