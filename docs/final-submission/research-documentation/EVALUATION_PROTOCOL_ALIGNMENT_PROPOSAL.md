## 3. Measures and analysis plan

Keep measures in separate constructs and report denominators, missingness and version. No single “accuracy” or “effectiveness” score should combine them.

| Measure | Operational definition | Analysis and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Task success | If observation is authorised: per-task unassisted success, assisted success, attempted-not-completed, skipped, technical failure and withdrawal as distinct states, against a task-specific observable endpoint. | Counts and denominators by task. Do not treat assistance as unassisted completion or a skip as failure. The original four-task sheet has no scoring rubric; build/approve a four-task rubric before observer scoring. |
| Completion time | If specifically authorised: elapsed time from instruction to completion/stop, with pauses, setup and technical outages recorded separately. | Descriptive per-task median/range (and IQR if useful); retain unsuccessful attempts separately. Do not compare time as learner ability or pool unlike tasks. Timing/observation was marked N/A in ethics form B4 and is not cleared by the mere presence of later blank sheets. |
| Assistance and errors | If authorised: neutral help given/requested and observable interaction errors tied to a predefined task code. | Descriptive counts/types and task notes, deidentified. Define assistance prompts and error coding before sessions; observer judgement and moderator allegiance are validity risks. |
| Explanation comprehension | Questionnaire Q20–Q23 responses and any approved, task-linked neutral probes. Q20–Q23 concern MET, missing evidence, conditional progression and checking official sources. | Report answer distributions and unanswered/not-applicable separately. Create an answer key from independently checked frozen source/rule evidence before scoring. The newer E1–E5/C1–C5 rubrics are not automatically the same instrument, and some probe topics exceed the four tasks. No validated learning-gain or total comprehension score. |
| Clarity/usefulness perceptions | Questionnaire Likert items, particularly Q10–Q19 and task-conditional items, with optional Q25–Q29 comments. | Item-level distributions and N/A/missing counts; no arbitrary composite, SUS claim, trust claim or educational benefit claim. Q19 is perceived usefulness; the instrument does not contain a validated trust scale. |
| Qualitative feedback | Optional open text, if optional omission is authorised. | Descriptive, versioned coding with negative/disconfirming cases retained; avoid saturation claims from a small exploratory sample. Do not solicit real education histories or identifying data. |
| Recommendation relevance | Separate method/order-blinded independent ratings of stated-interest/content fit for the fixed candidate set. | Only after valid independent ratings are collected and pool checks pass. Report per-judge/per-scenario measures under the separate protocol in §5. Not participant satisfaction, eligibility correctness or educational effectiveness. |

“MET / NOT_MET / NOT_ASSESSED” are system policy-assessment states, not user-research scores. Rule execution tests and source-grounded correctness checks must remain distinct from perceived relevance, comprehension and usefulness.

## 5. Independent relevance evaluation proposal

**Recommended working version:** use the revised Sprint 2B blinded A/B pair pack at `docs/evidence/research-sprint2/evaluation-readiness/relevance-review/{A,B}/`, with its matching blank response forms and `RELEVANCE_REVIEW_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, as the sole prospective pack candidate. Freeze exact hashes/version after supervisor/ethics review. It contains the same 127 unique scenario–programme pairs in two different permutations (not 254 pairs or judgements), with all 11 catalogue programmes represented. Two independent adult judges are proposed, but their selection, competence, independence, burden and procedure remain unapproved. No ratings have been collected.

The pack contains 21 scenarios: nine development-exposed and 12 evaluator-authored, catalogue-aware follow-ups. These are not independent held-out scenarios. D01 empty-interest and D08 uninterpretable-interest robustness cases (five pairs each) are excluded from relevance metrics in advance, leaving at most 117 pairs across 19 interpretable scenarios before further missingness, zero-gain or candidate-group exclusions. Cards present interests and frozen programme text, not ranking methods, scores or ranks; public programme content is identifiable. This is method/order blinding, not institution anonymity. The researcher key and comparator outputs must remain withheld until initial ratings are locked.

Use the stated 0–3 content-relevance anchors plus `UNJUDGEABLE`; blank means skipped, not zero. Each judge rates independently. Preserve per-judge ratings, missingness and a 4×4 disagreement table; do not average ordinal ratings. Under the saved analysis specification, calculate nDCG only for completely judged candidate groups with nonzero ideal gain, within the same MET / NOT_ASSESSED / NOT_MET partition, using nDCG@3 for groups of at least three, nDCG@2 for groups of two, and exclude singleton groups from ordering summaries. Exclude D01/D08 as predeclared. Compare methods only on identical complete-case pools; report per judge and scenario/group, and separate development-exposed from follow-up scenarios. No statistical superiority or generalisation claim is appropriate for this small correlated catalogue-aware set.

Do not use the older 51-pair A/B pack and current 127-pair pack concurrently. Independent rating would make labels independent of developer rankings, but would not make the scenarios independent or held out. Do not use the single generic 127-row `docs/evidence/research-sprint2/ai-ranking/BLANK_RELEVANCE_REVIEW.csv` as a second pack; it lacks the same pack-specific response identity fields and duplicates the candidate-pair pool conceptually.

## 6. Study boundaries

The minimum proposed usability study is the four-task single-system sequence above plus the corresponding questionnaire, subject to exact ethics clearance. Do not add the source-lookup comparison from `EVALUATION_PLAN_v2.md` / `COMPARISON_TASKS_v2.md`: it is a different, proposed within-person study with S1/S2 conditions and counterbalancing, not part of the four-task baseline. Different scenarios across conditions, carryover, source changes and small sample constrain causal interpretation even if later approved.

Do not add six-card tasks that introduce a failed Engineering case, NVQ progression, skills/scholarship/chatbot use, multilingual switching, keyboard accessibility or voice. These are useful system demonstrations and future evaluation candidates, but expand participant burden and research constructs. Objective VI and implemented features do not establish human testing approval. Native browser speech may involve external processing and remains outside this core proposal.

The independent relevance pack is a distinct research activity. Do not ask usability participants to judge algorithm outputs as if they were independent judges, and do not infer user trust from relevance scores.
