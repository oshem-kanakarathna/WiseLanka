## Prioritised critical academic weaknesses

**Submission blockers**

- The actual approved proposal, ethics, interim, supervisor feedback and assessment/rubric/poster/viva documents have not been inspected. Supplied excerpts restore questions, not documentary verification or institution-specific compliance.
- ObjectiveVII explicitly includes usability feedback. No eligible adult participant work has been collected. It cannot be marked complete through automated tests. Do not recruit until signed conditions and all missing data-management/consent fields are checked.
- SRQ-II and SRQ-IV ask about improvement. Current evidence contains no human baseline/comparison, dedicated trust measure or native/fluent accessibility findings. Even a completed one-condition clarity questionnaire would not demonstrate causal improvement. Preserve the questions and report limited/descriptive answers, or obtain approval for a justified comparison design.

**Major weaknesses before final academic closure**

- ObjectiveI is only partly supported by the current narrative. Abstract-led appraisal is insufficient for detailed methodological comparisons or a strong claim of a research gap. Broaden career-guidance/context and compare a small set of actual systems, using full texts where possible.
- ObjectiveII/current Sri Lankan learner challenges have limited empirical/context evidence. Historical NEC research and personal motivation do not describe current prevalence or the needs of every target category.
- Relevance effectiveness (SRQ-V) is unmeasured independently: no legitimate relevance labels, nDCG or superiority result. Nine synthetic ranker cases show behaviour, not quality. Independent labels may require ethical review; no invented ratings.
- Educational source fidelity is selectively reviewed, not independently established across the catalogue. Reported awards are not authenticated; recognition/career-skill relationships have uneven provenance. Structural integrity does not validate their educational meaning.
- Sinhala/Tamil fluency, native speech, screen-reader/full-keyboard/zoom/target-device behaviour remain pending. Zero axe violations in selected states is neither full accessibility nor WCAG conformance.

**Presentation and analytical refinements**

Keep the source-policy correction as the central evaluated design lesson; reduce repeated caveats by cross-reference while retaining limits near important claims. Replace process/status prose with author-owned scholarly analysis at final editing. Explain alternatives through requirements and evidence rather than popularity. No extra word count compensates for missing full-text appraisal or observations. The current DSR mapping is retrospective and should be supported by genuine dated decisions, not a fabricated research diary.
