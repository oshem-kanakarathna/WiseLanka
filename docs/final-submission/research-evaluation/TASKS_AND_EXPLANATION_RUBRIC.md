## Objective comprehension probes (after relevant task)

Use neutral questions; record the actual response before scoring. Scores are a project-specific rubric, not a validated psychometric scale.

| Probe | Question | Correct content | 0 / 1 / 2 scoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | What does NOT_ASSESSED mean here? | Evidence/provider decision remains unresolved; it is neither a rejection nor confirmation of eligibility | 0 incorrect;1 mentions unknown vaguely;2 explicitly distinguishes both |
| E2 | Does a high content-similarity score mean likely admission? | No; interests/catalogue text similarity is separate from academic conditions and admission | 0 probability/admission claim;1 says no without reason;2 explains separation |
| E3 | What must happen before this conditional vocational progression can be confirmed? | Recorded qualification route plus provider document/admission checks; no automatic exemption | 0 guaranteed;1 partial condition;2 relevant route and provider uncertainty |
| E4 | An unreported career skill is shown. What can the system conclude? | No competence conclusion; optional self-report only | 0 assumes lack;1 recognises uncertainty;2 explains no objective skill assessment |
| E5 | Is the recorded scholarship necessarily open now? | No; dates/status must be checked independently of personal eligibility | 0 assumes available;1 says check;2 distinguishes availability and eligibility |

Report each probe's distribution and denominator, including unanswered/N/A. Do not invent a validated pass threshold or sum into a “learning gain”. If multiple raters score answers, define training on separate examples, preserve initial scores and report disagreement/adjudication. Ratings by the implementation author alone must be disclosed as such.
