# Adult evaluation package — prepared for ethics/supervisor review

Version 0.1, 29 October 2026. **NOT RELEASED FOR RECRUITMENT.** No participants recruited, personal data collected or results generated. The package is structurally prepared for review but cannot be called ready to submit to an ethics committee until its required local fields and institutional forms are supplied.

## Purpose and proposed procedures

Evaluate task usability and explanation comprehension of the frozen WiseLanka prototype using supplied fictional learner profiles. Proposed session: approximately 25–35 minutes, adjustable after approved piloting. Intended participants are consenting adults aged18+; approved recruitment channel, number, inclusion criteria and compensation (if any) are PENDING. Do not recruit minors or infer signed approval from this draft. No personal learning histories or real certificate details are needed.

The observer records a study ID, task completion, assistance, time/errors, language/browser and deidentified comments. Participant information and consent materials are in the companion file. The custom questionnaire measures reported clarity/usability, not validated clinical or competence constructs. Explanation checks have an explicit answer rubric. Participation is voluntary; skip/stop without penalty. Avoid recruitment through dependent teaching/assessment relationships unless specifically approved.

## Data-management fields requiring approval

Researcher and supervisor contacts; ethics reference/conditions; approved recruitment text/site; data controller/contact route; storage location/encryption/access list; consent-record handling; retention/deletion schedule; withdrawal deadline and linkage method; analysis/publication permissions; complaint route: **ALL PENDING APPROVED DOCUMENTS**. Do not upload real study data into Git, public issue trackers or AI prompts. Blank templates are versioned; completed participant records belong only in the approved restricted store. If quotes are used, check indirect identifiability.

## Risk and mitigation review

| Potential risk | Proposed mitigation | Approval dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Guidance mistaken for real admission advice | Fictional profiles; clear prototype/non-guarantee/source disclaimers | Review information sheet and task wording |
| Sensitive data entered voluntarily | No real grades/identifiers requested; moderator reminder; approved redaction process | Confirm incident/redaction arrangements |
| Language misunderstanding | Draft strings labelled; fluent review before language-dependent research | Record actual reviewer competence, no invented certification |
| Voice audio leaves browser | Exclude native participant voice by default; typing/mock demonstration only | Separate approval/optional informed consent for actual third-party processing |
| Distress, fatigue or perceived pressure | Skip/stop/break without penalty; no performance judgement | Confirm recruitment/withdrawal terms |
| Reidentification in small sample | Minimum data, separate consent, aggregate reports, deidentify quotes | Approved storage/retention and access conditions |

## Review checklist

Verify signed ethics scope and adult condition; fill missing fields; use required institutional forms; approve tasks/questionnaire/data plan; review translated information/consent if needed; authorise any pilot; assign version/date; only then recruit. Changes to data collection require the applicable amendment process. No native microphone test is necessary for the standard usability session.
