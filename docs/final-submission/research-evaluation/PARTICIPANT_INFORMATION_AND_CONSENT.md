## Proposed participant information wording

You are invited to evaluate WiseLanka, a University of West London final-year
prototype for education and career guidance. The proposed session lasts about
25–35 minutes. You will use fictional learner details supplied by the researcher,
complete interface tasks and optionally answer questions about usability and
explanations. We are evaluating the software, not your educational ability.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question, stop or decline without
penalty. Use synthetic details only: do not enter your real grades, certificate
numbers, name, income or other sensitive information. Guidance is incomplete and
is not an admission decision. Confirm real educational decisions with providers.

The researcher proposes to record task completion, elapsed time, errors, requests
for help and deidentified comments under a study ID. No audio/video recording is
proposed. Recordings or additional data would require explicit ethics authorisation.
Storage, retention and withdrawal arrangements must be completed above and explained
before consent. Anonymous data may not be removable after irreversible aggregation;
the approved withdrawal arrangements must clearly address that point.

Native browser speech can transmit audio to a browser-provider service. Do not
activate it in participant research unless the ethics approval explicitly permits
that processing and separate optional consent is approved. Otherwise demonstrate
mocked voice states or use typing. Refusal to use voice must not prevent participation.

Sinhala/Tamil strings are drafts. Fluent-language review should precede a study
which depends on their accuracy; do not require participants to rely on unreviewed
translations for real decisions.

## Moderator task cards (proposed, not executed)

Use the frozen scenarios.json in the parent directory. Do not reveal expected answers
until after observing the task. Counterbalance language/order where feasible.

T1: Enter OL6; find its foundation route and official source. Explain what is met
and why progression is not guaranteed.
T2: Enter AL_IT; identify the subject evidence and the missing UGC admission/selection
checks. Explain why high semantic similarity cannot establish eligibility.
T3: Enter the Business or failed-Engineering scenario; locate the source and distinguish an
unmet requirement from an unassessed provider decision.
T4: Enter NVQ6; explore conditional progression, identify its conditions and state
whether equivalence/admission has been verified.
T5: Use guided support for skills/careers and the recorded closed scholarship.
Identify self-report limits, source coverage and current application status.
T6: Switch English/Sinhala/Tamil without losing results. Use keyboard navigation;
use typed assistant input when voice is unavailable. Native voice is optional and
only included if approved; language-support failures count as observations.

Record task success independently of agreeing with a recommendation. Mark assisted
success separately from unassisted completion. Stop timers during researcher setup
or technical outages and record the exclusion reason.

## Custom questionnaire (not a validated SUS instrument)

Use 1 strongly disagree … 5 strongly agree, plus N/A and optional comments.
Q1: I could enter the supplied learner information.
Q2: I could find official sources for the guidance.
Q3: I understood the distinction between eligibility and similarity.
Q4: I could identify information the system had not assessed.
Q5: I understood the conditions on alternative/progression routes.
Q6: The interface language and error feedback were understandable.
Q7: I could continue using text when voice was unavailable or unwanted.
Q8: I understood that self-reported skills were not verified competence.

Open questions: What was unclear? Which explanation helped? What would prevent you
from using this prototype? Avoid requesting personal histories or sensitive data.

Optional SUS: use a properly sourced, unmodified standard 10-item form only after
supervisor/ethics approval. Do not call the custom eight-item survey SUS. Standard
scoring uses odd-item response minus 1 and 5 minus even-item response, sums ten
contributions and multiplies by 2.5. A resulting 0–100 score is not a percentage.
No SUS score is currently available; missing/modified items invalidate standard
scoring. Obtain the official instrument and justification before choosing it.

## Planned analysis (not findings)

Describe recruitment, sample size, withdrawals, missing observations and languages
actually used. Report unassisted and assisted completion n/N, errors and task-time
median/range or IQR. For custom Likert items, show response distributions and N/A
counts; avoid inventing validated thresholds. Summarise explanation-comprehension
errors separately from satisfaction. Use a documented coding table for comments,
preserving negative/disconfirming observations and deidentifying quotations.

A convenience sample supports exploratory conclusions only. No causal claim,
population-wide satisfaction estimate or improvement in educational outcomes follows
from a small usability study. Report translation, order, moderator and selection
biases. Do not use usability ratings to tune a ranker and then report them as
independent test evidence.
