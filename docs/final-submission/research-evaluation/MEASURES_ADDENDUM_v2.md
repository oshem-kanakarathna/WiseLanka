# Proposed instrument/participant-information addendum v0.2

Not ethics-approved. Read alongside PARTICIPANT_INFORMATION_AND_CONSENT.md. Preserve previously approved versions; do not administer these additional items/procedures until conditions/amendment needs are checked.

## Plain-language addition for participant review

“If approved, you will use official source pages and WiseLanka for two fictional guidance tasks. The order is varied across participants. We compare how the tools help you find and interpret information; we are not testing your ability. We propose to record completion, time, assistance and deidentified answers. You may skip, stop or decline without disadvantage. No real learner records or audio/video are required. The software is a prototype, not an admissions authority.”

Approved contacts, data storage/retention and withdrawal terms must be completed in the main sheet. The proposed revised session estimate is35–40minutes, replacing the earlier25–35minute draft estimate only if approved. Final burden must match the approved information sheet. This addendum is not consent by itself. Proposed optional checkbox: “I understand that the study may involve both official-source lookup and WiseLanka tasks, with order varied.” Do not pre-tick; university wording may differ.

## After each comparison condition

Use1 strongly disagree–5 strongly agree, plusN/A and optional reason. Same wording for A and B: “this method” means the condition just used. Do not call these validated scales; report items separately.

- U1: This method helped me find guidance relevant to the fictional learner's stated goal.
- U2: I could identify the academic requirements that were met, unmet or unresolved.
- P1: I could see which evidence supported the guidance.
- P2: I could identify the limits or missing information in the guidance.
- T1: I would rely on this method as a starting point for further investigation.
- T2: I would check unresolved entry conditions with an official provider before acting.
- C1: I found this method easy to use for the task.

T2 measures stated verification intention, not “more trust”; do not reverse-score it into a trust total. T1 measures bounded reported reliance, not calibrated trust or actual behaviour. Compare with comprehension probes and the prompt below. Keep original eight-item WiseLanka questionnaire as a separate post-system instrument; mark items not applicable rather than forcing answers about source-only lookup.

After each task, ask neutrally: “What conclusion can this fictional learner draw, and what should they check next?” Record before correcting. Use E1/E2 (and E3/E4 on pathways/skills tasks) with the existing0–2 rubric. Post-task confidence0–100 is optional and needs approval; if used, it is confidence in that answer, not an admission probability. Do not infer validated calibration from one response.

## Planning and usability debrief

After T3–T5: “What possible next step did you identify?”, “Which conditions still need confirmation?”, “Did this help with the fictional plan, and what remained unclear?” Probe an unreported skill without implying failure. After T6: “Which controls/content were difficult to understand or access?” Request only deidentified comments; do not elicit personal education/financial histories.

Report clear/unclear and helpful/unhelpful responses as collected. Positive perceived usefulness is not proof of better decisions. No SUS, trust gain, accessibility improvement or learning outcome is currently calculated.
