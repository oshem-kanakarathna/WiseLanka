# Independent ranking judgement protocol — frozen retrospective evaluation

**Proposed; no human judgements collected, no nDCG calculated.** Confirm applicable ethics conditions for human judging before recruitment. This is evaluation of the nine existing development scenarios, not a held-out test or proof of generalisation. No algorithm/corpus tuning after seeing ratings is permitted in this evaluation.

1. Freeze code/data/model and `docs/evidence/wp5/comparison.json`. Record hashes, model revision and actual candidate pools. Run `python3 docs/final-submission/tools/prepare_ranking_review.py` from repository root; it prepares two independently randomised method-blinded packs and blank sheets. The manifest records9 cases and51 unique case/candidate pairs. Each packet contains51 possible responses; two packets imply102 possible judgements, not102 participants or completed ratings.
2. Seek two consenting adult judges who did not implement/tune the system or author the scenarios; ideally one with relevant educational/domain knowledge and one with recommendation/research competence. Record actual expertise/relationship/conflicts under approved handling. Being a supervisor alone does not prove independence. If only one judge is available, disclose that and do not claim inter-rater reliability. These judges may have different perspectives from learners; usability usefulness is separately measured.
3. Give each judge PROTOCOL.md, their rater-cards-A/B.md and ratings-A/B-blank.csv only. Withhold comparison.json, researcher-key.json, model/method scores and predicted rank. The programme title/source is visible; anonymising method does not anonymise educational content. Judge independently before any discussion. Instructions: relevance is fit to the fictional stated interests/career goal, not eligibility, prestige or admission likelihood.
4. Use0=unrelated to stated goal,1=weak/indirect fit,2=clear related route,3=strong direct fit. Record a short reason and UNJUDGEABLE when intent/evidence cannot support a judgement. Do not force ratings on empty/uninterpretable goals. Cold-start `empty` remains a robustness case, excluded from preference-quality metrics; `unfamiliar` may be unjudgeable. Preserve source access failures/contradictions separately; do not silently revise the frozen descriptors. Incomplete/unjudgeable records are not zero.
5. Collect approved deidentified files in restricted storage, not Git. Verify pair coverage and legal grades/duplicates before joining the researcher key. Lock initial ratings before revealing methods; preserve originals, disagreement and adjudication. Report exact agreement counts and disagreements by case; optional weighted agreement only if justified with sufficient data, never invented. Primary analysis: calculate results separately for each judge on their own completely rated candidate pools; show sensitivity across judges. Any consensus results are secondary and must disclose the documented adjudication rule. Do not average ordinal ratings without a declared rationale.
6. Compare saved **grouped deterministic, TF–IDF and MiniLM** orders on identical candidates within the fixed academic groups. The saved `legacy_original` order is preserved separately as a descriptive historical baseline. Never reorder across groups for a relevance metric or silently substitute grouped order while calling it raw legacy order. Report the NOT_ASSESSED/NOT_MET composition and group sizes; most cases do not demonstrate eligible admissions.
7. For group size n≥3 compute nDCG@3 with gain2^rating−1 and discountlog2(rank+1). For n=2, label nDCG@2, not@3; for singleton groups disclose triviality and omit from comparative macro-average. Use the ideal ordering of the same completely judged group. If ideal gain is0 or any label is missing/UNJUDGEABLE, output N/A with the reason. Do not let each method use a different subset of rated candidates. The fixed k=min(3,n) convention is therefore reported with its actual k.
8. Show per-scenario/group/judge scores, candidate counts, evaluable/excluded counts and paired method differences, with a macro-average across nontrivial comparable units only if its weighting is declared. Singleton counts and zero-gain cases must remain visible. Small correlated scenario groups do not justify automatic significance or population superiority claims. Preserve differences where TF–IDF/deterministic performs better. No nDCG can be reported until actual independent ratings pass coverage checks.

The existing scenarios were exposed during development. Independent rating now improves label independence, not scenario independence. Any later held-out study must freeze new cases before tuning, with no use of their judgements for selection. No feedback training occurs in this project. Programme relevance is not user satisfaction, source correctness or educational effectiveness.

Graded rank evaluation is motivated by [Järvelin and Kekäläinen (2002)](https://researchportal.tuni.fi/en/publications/cumulated-gain-based-evaluation-of-ir-techniques/). The gain/group/exclusion rules above are the declared project protocol, not a claim of exact replication. Results currently: **NONE**.
