## Interpretation

The development is cumulative: August established the layered rule-driven pathway artifact; October strengthened evidence semantics and coverage, introduced actual local pretrained inference and implemented bounded interaction/access features. Evaluation discovered limitations in earlier outputs. Increased test counts alone are weaker evidence than the demonstrated source-policy correction and preserved uncertainty. The current205 tests include new/revised policy expectations; they cannot be treated as an unchanged benchmark proving a percentage improvement over40.

The interim uses broad terms such as personalised and AI-powered for the overall project. Its implemented screenshots show rules/match scores; final writing must separate that stage from October MiniLM inference. Likewise, July/August source dates, interim screenshots and current WP5 logs have different evidential roles. No October feature or participant result is retroactively attributed to the interim.
