## Claim and quality controls applied

- The exact approved aim/objectives are preserved in appendix A; exact main/supporting questions are restored from author-supplied text; detailed NFRs are not reconstructed.
- Critical analysis distinguishes code correctness from source fidelity, semantic proximity from relevance, and satisfaction from educational outcomes.
- Architecture and alternatives match actual modules; no database, paid service, automatic feedback training or framework migration is implied.
- Runtime figures identify warm request context, platform and process RSS; model loading excludes download and full cold-machine startup.
- Source checks are scoped; publisher abstracts are not presented as full-text literature appraisals. Stored catalogue verification dates are not new WP6 access claims.
- Negative/initial-failure records remain in the WP5 checkpoint. Revised expectations are linked to source-policy changes.
- Human-data templates remain empty, manual checks pending, and translation/speech caveats explicit.
- AI drafting assistance is disclosed. Module permission/declaration requirements remain to be checked; no signed originality declaration is invented.
- Poster and viva use the same counts/model terminology; no satisfaction/nDCG/accuracy uplift is invented.

1. Inspect missing authoritative documents and reconcile structure, word limit, question mapping, rubric and permitted assistance.
