# Proposed independent review — unadministered

Confirm applicable ethics approval and the actual instrument/recruitment conditions before contacting judges. Ideally obtain two appropriately qualified adults independent of implementation/tuning; do not claim that a supervisor or developer is independent merely because they can rate a card. No human work has occurred.

Give each judge only their assigned CARDS_A/B.md, matching BLANK_A/B.csv and this instruction sheet. Withhold the researcher key, comparator outputs, method identities, ranks, numeric model scores and analysis. Scenario/programme identities can be reconstructed from the public content, so this is method/order blinding, not anonymisation of institutions.

Judge the programme's content fit to the fictional stated interests/career intention, not admission likelihood, prestige or a presumed algorithm answer. Use0 unrelated,1 weak/indirect,2 clear related,3 strong direct; useUNJUDGEABLE if intent/evidence is insufficient. Preserve a reason and source-access problems. Empty/noise intents are robustness-only and must not be forced into relevance scores. Facts are the frozen short catalogue fields; source changes/extra information must be documented rather than silently substituted.

Work independently before discussing disagreement. Lock initial ratings before revealing method identities; preserve originals, missingness, conflicts and any later adjudication. Each127-pair pack represents potential responses, not participants or achieved ratings. Store actual completed data only under approved restricted handling, not Git or AI prompts.

Later analysis must compare methods on identical fully judged candidate groups, preserve academic partitions, disclose singleton/zero-gain/unjudgeable exclusions and distinguish relevance from learner satisfaction. Existing development cases and evaluator-authored follow-ups remain their original provenance after rating; independent ratings do not create an independently designed held-out test. No metric may be computed until legitimate labels exist and the final analysis rule is approved/frozen. No nDCG/MAP/precision/accuracy is available from this sprint.
