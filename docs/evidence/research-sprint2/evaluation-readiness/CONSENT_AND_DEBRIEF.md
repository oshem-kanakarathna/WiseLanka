Use the university-required approved form when confirmed.

## Consent record — completed privately only after clearance

Study code: ______  Track (usability / independent relevance): ______

Please tick each required agreement yourself. No boxes are preselected.

- [ ] I confirm I am 18 or older (no birth date requested).
- [ ] I read/understood the information sheet and could ask questions.
- [ ] I understand the procedures and this is fictional prototype evaluation, not admissions advice.
- [ ] I understand participation is voluntary and I may omit questions, pause or stop without penalty.

- [ ] I understand signed consent is separate from response data and no unnecessary personal details should be entered.

Optional, separate permission: deidentified short quotations in academic outputs [ ] Yes [ ] No. Leaving this blank means no quotation permission, not refusal of the whole study.

Participant signature: ______ Date: ______ Researcher signature: ______ Date: ______
Keep signatures in the approved restricted consent store, not analysis CSVs or Git. No name, email, date of birth or real learner record is requested on response templates. Moderator verifies all applicable required agreements; do not start if absent. Give the participant a copy and code. Never record an invented signature or a default “yes”.

## Withdrawal procedure

During a session stop immediately on request; ask only whether already collected responses may be retained, without pressure. Default to removing them if permission is unclear. Use the approved retention/deletion rules for required consent records separately. After the session use the code and approved private contact route, not a public repository. Log date, code and processing status privately; do not ask for a reason. Confirm deletion from active data and handle backups according to the approved policy. Apply the approved cutoff only if expressly authorised; do not improvise one.

## Debrief wording — read after tasks or on early exit

“Thank you. We are evaluating the clarity and usefulness of WiseLanka, not your ability. These learners were fictional. Academic eligibility, interest similarity and provider admission decisions are different. Some facts or selection conditions remain unknown. Self-reported skills are not verified competence. Your comments, including criticism, help us understand limitations; they do not establish educational effectiveness. You can ask questions or request withdrawal through the route and timing on your information sheet.”

Ask whether they want to add or remove a comment; do not press. Provide researcher/supervisor and independent complaint contacts from the approved sheet. Do not disclose method comparisons to a relevance judge before all initial ratings are locked; explain this temporary withholding in advance and provide a later methods debrief if approved. No deception about actual admissions is permitted.
