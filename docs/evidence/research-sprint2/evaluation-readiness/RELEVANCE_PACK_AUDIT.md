# Relevance-pack audit — observations and revisions

Inspected the original Sprint 2B cards A/B, both response sheets, researcher key, frozen profiles/candidates, three comparator outputs, catalogue text and analysis script. Original files remain unchanged.

- Both packs have exactly **127 unique scenario–programme pairs**; no within-pack duplicates or missing/extra candidates. A/B are permutations of the same pairs, not 254 independent observations. Composite identity includes pack, case and candidate: S001/C001 alone is ambiguous across packs.
- All 11 active programmes occur. Five programmes occur 20 times each (PRG0001/2/3/8/9); PRG0004 occurs seven times; PRG0005/6/7/10/11 four times each. Coverage is imbalanced and is not a catalogue-wide quality census. Repeated programmes across different scenarios are intentional.
- All 127 archived academic states agree with the partitions saved for every comparator. States remain research metadata, not reviewer relevance labels; no pack response implies eligibility. The original cards show no admission guarantee.
- Method identities, output ranks and scores do not appear as labels in the original cards/sheets. Randomised display order is not method order. Programme titles containing “Artificial Intelligence” describe programmes, not the evaluated method. Public programme identities are recognisable: this is method/order blinding, not institutional anonymity.
- **Risk:** full fictional qualification profiles could prime admission judgements although the requested construct is interest/content fit. Revised cards below retain only the frozen interest query and the exact programme text. Original profiles/statuses remain in researcher metadata; this does not change computational inputs.
- **Risk:** live official URLs may yield unequal information or updated content; original cards cannot isolate that influence. Revised judge cards use only frozen text and opaque programme IDs; original URLs/source IDs remain in the researcher register. Judge only what is supplied; insufficient detail warrants UNJUDGEABLE. This is sparse-text relevance, not expert review of full curricula.
- **Risk:** “suitable” could blend content, prestige and admission. Revised instructions define one construct, neutral ordinal anchors and unrelated fictional examples. They do not mention observed method performance or promote AI. No preassigned target programme or example from the measured cases is supplied.
- Two robustness cases D01 (empty interest) and D08 (uninterpretable vocabulary), five candidates each, remain in the 127-pair audit and blank instruments. They are excluded from relevance metrics in advance. Thus at most **117 pairs across 19 interpretable scenarios** are potentially relevance-assessable; missing labels/zero ideal gain/singletons reduce informative comparisons further.
- Nine cases were development-exposed; 12 are evaluator-authored, catalogue-aware follow-ups frozen before Sprint 2B. Neither set is independently held out. Independent judging would add independent labels, not independent scenario construction.

Revised A/B cards preserve opaque pair mappings and permutations. Use two independent adult judges with appropriate programme/career familiarity and no contribution to implementation/tuning, subject to approval. Disclose expertise and conflicts using broad role categories rather than CVs or identifiers. Workload is 127 cards per judge, proposed two blocks with breaks; estimate 45–75 minutes, to be confirmed by an approved pilot. Decline/skip/withdraw remains available.

The key and source/status metadata are in RESEARCHER_ONLY_PAIR_REGISTER.csv. Withhold it and all comparator outputs, analysis and original full-profile cards until independent ratings are locked. Record any prior access as a blinding breach; do not silently discard unfavourable responses.
