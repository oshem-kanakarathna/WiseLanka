# Proposed minimum-data protocol — approval required

Response templates contain study/reviewer code, instrument version, task/probe/item ID, browser/OS family and input language when relevant; no names, real grades, income, account identifiers, exact location or birthdays. Record adult eligibility as consent confirmation, not date of birth. Broad reviewer expertise/conflict declarations belong in the restricted study log; avoid identifying institutions/employers. No participant data are collected now.

Separate stores: (1) signed consent/contact/withdrawal linkage under approved restricted access; (2) coded raw responses and observation sheets; (3) deidentified analysis outputs. While code-based withdrawal is possible, call the data pseudonymised. Consent signatures are personal data even if no name field is present. Use the actual approved university storage, encryption, access list, transfer channel, backup/deletion schedule and retention period: **all require confirmation**. Do not invent a retention period, storage service, legal basis or withdrawal cutoff. Block release until those fields are completed. Any necessary recruitment contacts stay separate and are removed according to the approved schedule.

Provide study codes without embedding identity. Preserve raw responses read-only, version cleaned derivatives and log edits with reasons; never replace missing ratings with an algorithm answer. Keep the card-permutation key research-only, separate from judge materials. Actual completed templates, signatures, contact lists and quotations remain outside the repository, public forms, AI prompts and third-party analytics. No audio/video/screen capture; do not activate native speech during study sessions. A microphone amendment needs separate optional consent and provider-processing review.

Record task times/errors without copying accidental personal entries. If personal information is entered, pause and follow the approved incident/redaction procedure; do not retain screenshots or export browser logs containing it. Researcher notes should paraphrase without identifiers. Quotations need optional permission and indirect-identifiability review. Aggregate small cells or omit granular combinations that could identify someone. Report actual missingness/withdrawals, not invented sample numbers.

Withdrawal follows CONSENT_AND_DEBRIEF.md and the approved information sheet. The participant retains their code; remove linked response records on an eligible request, log action privately, and handle backups under the approved policy. Explain irreversible anonymisation limits only when approved. No signed records are to be destroyed contrary to university retention obligations; resolve this before recruitment. No promise of immediate deletion from every backup is made.
