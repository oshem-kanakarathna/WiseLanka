# Adult usability task cards — draft, no sessions executed

Proposed six to eight adult volunteers after supervisor approval; convenience sample, exploratory only. Adults should understand the information language and be able to give informed consent. Do not recruit under-18s, vulnerable groups or students subject to researcher assessment. Exact number, recruitment channel and accessibility arrangements require approval. No screening for actual grades or sensitive history. Separate card-review judges from usability participants where feasible to avoid exposure.

Proposed session 25–35 minutes, English, typing only, no recording. Allow breaks and skipping. Explain that the system is being tested, not the participant. Give one task at a time and its fictional input from TASK_INPUT_CARDS.md. Do not reveal the researcher answer rubric or SYNTHETIC_TASK_INPUTS.json (which contains moderator-only expected fields). Use a clean browser/profile between fictional learners; verify stale results are cleared. Record actual order, browser/device and version without device identifiers. All task times are descriptive, not a race.

| Task | Participant instruction | Input |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | Enter the supplied O/L results. Find the Computing foundation option, its academic assessment and official information. Explain in your own words what the result lets you conclude. | OL6 |
| U2 | Replace the profile with the supplied A/L learner and stated IT interest. Find one IT option and explain the academic result, relevance information and what still needs checking. | AL_IT + career intention “Software engineering and computer science” |
| U3 | Use the supplied Engineering learner. Find the relevant programme, explain any qualification gap and identify a reasonable next step using the displayed evidence. | AL_Engineering_gap |
| U4 | Use the supplied vocational qualification. Explore the Mechatronics progression route and tell us what must be checked before proceeding. | NVQ6 |
| U5 | Return to the supplied IT profile. Choose the supplied Software Engineer career and report only Programming as a fictional self-reported skill. Find the skill insights, then ask the guided assistant about the programme or pathway. Explain what you understand. | AL_IT, career CAR0001, skill SKL0001 |

After U2/U3 ask the corresponding comprehension questions; after U4/U5 ask the others. After tasks give the optional questionnaire. Moderator may offer neutral help after the participant requests it; record assistance and do not count this as unassisted success. Proposed soft limit five minutes per task: ask whether they want help, continue or stop; overruns are logged rather than silently censored. System outages pause timing and are separate from participant errors. Exact limits/order require approval and pilot confirmation.

This minimum session does not compare a search engine or alternate ranking modes. Optional ordinary-source lookup/counterbalanced comparative tasks in previous drafts are not authorised by this plan and require separate reviewed materials. Single-condition observations cannot establish a causal improvement in usefulness, trust or understanding. No compulsory language switching or microphone use: manual native-language/voice validation remains a separate pending activity.
