# Optional post-task questions — custom, unvalidated instrument

For each Q1–Q9, use 1 strongly disagree, 2 disagree, 3 neither agree nor disagree, 4 agree, 5 strongly agree, or N/A. You may omit any question. These are perceptions, not measured educational benefits or a validated trust/accessibility scale. Do not enter personal histories or identifiers.

- Q1: The profile-entry steps were clear.
- Q2: I could find the information needed for the tasks.
- Q3: The relevance information was understandable.
- Q4: The explanation made clear what was known and unknown.
- Q5: The displayed guidance was useful for the fictional task.
- Q6: I could understand the conditions on the alternative route.
- Q7: I could navigate the interface using my preferred controls.
- Q8: The guidance made it clear what still required provider confirmation.
- Q9: I would feel confident using this prototype as one source of information when planning what to check next.

Optional open questions:
- O1: What, if anything, was unclear or difficult?
- O2: What did the eligibility or relevance explanation help you understand, if anything?
- O3: What would you want to check before relying on this guidance?
- O4: How did the alternative-pathway and skills information affect your understanding, if at all?
- O5: What would you change about the interface or guidance?

Do not interpret Q9 alone as justified trust. The moderator compares its interpretation with the separate comprehension answers. This is not SUS; no summed score or normative pass threshold is specified.
