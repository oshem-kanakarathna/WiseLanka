# Participant information sheet — draft for ethics review

Study: WiseLanka prototype guidance evaluation. Researcher: [approved name/contact]. Supervisor: [approved name/contact]. University: University of West London. Ethics approval reference, approved version/date and independent complaint contact: **[TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE RELEASE]**.

We are studying how a prototype presents educational pathways, explanations and interests-based recommendations. We invite consenting adults aged 18 or above; no children, vulnerable adults or dependent student/teacher recruitment is proposed. You need not disclose your real grades, qualifications, income or career history. This research does not assess your ability or give an admission decision. Only supplied fictional learners are used.

There are two separate tracks. You will be told which invitation applies. **Usability:** approximately 25–35 minutes using fictional tasks and optional questions about clarity, usefulness, transparency and confidence; the researcher proposes to observe completion, time, help requests and misunderstandings. **Independent relevance review:** appropriately familiar adult reviewers assess short programme cards for content fit, proposed 45–75 minutes in two blocks. No algorithm outputs are shown. These tracks are not assumed covered by the same approval; the supervisor must confirm both. Proposed recruitment/number/venue/payment: [APPROVED DETAILS REQUIRED; no payment assumed].

Participation is voluntary. You can decline, skip questions, take breaks or stop without giving a reason or affecting studies/services. We will request written consent and, for usability, explicit agreement to observation. No audio, video, screen or native microphone recording is proposed. Use typed input; browser voice is excluded because it may send audio to a service provider. The standard study is English; fluent Sinhala/Tamil review and approved translated consent are needed before language-dependent research. These exclusions are not evidence that language, voice or accessibility features have been evaluated.

You may find tasks confusing or tiring. Tell the moderator, pause or stop. Use the prototype only for this fictional task and confirm real educational decisions with providers. Avoid entering personal information. Optional comments should not identify anyone. You may decline quotation permission and still participate.

Written consent signatures are identifiable and will be held separately from coded responses. While responses remain linked to a study code they are **pseudonymised**, not fully anonymous. Feedback is for academic research/assessment only under the approved consent. Only deidentified summaries and approved non-identifying quotations may appear in the dissertation/presentation.

We will confirm removal under the approved procedure. After irreversible aggregation/anonymisation, individual data cannot necessarily be isolated. This limit must be approved and explained before consent; the existing form promises withdrawal but supplies no cutoff. If approval does not permit a limit, do not aggregate irreversibly until the supervisor resolves it. Retain your study code and a copy of this sheet.
