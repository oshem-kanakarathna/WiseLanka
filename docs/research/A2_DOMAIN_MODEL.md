# A2 – WiseLanka Domain Model

## Version

0.1 (Draft)

## Project Identity

**WiseLanka** is an **AI-Powered Education Pathway Intelligence Platform for Sri Lanka**.

## Project Motto

> **Know More. Choose Better.**

## Purpose

WiseLanka was inspired by a real challenge experienced by the developer as a Sri Lankan learner.

While planning higher education and career pathways, it was difficult to discover all the available programmes, alternative pathways, scholarships and eligibility criteria from a single reliable source. As a result, valuable opportunities were missed and important educational decisions were delayed due to a lack of accessible and verified information.

The purpose of WiseLanka is to solve this problem by providing a centralised, AI-powered education and career intelligence platform that enables learners to discover educational pathways, understand eligibility requirements, explore alternative routes, identify scholarships and make informed decisions based on verified information.
