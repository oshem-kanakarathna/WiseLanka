# A3 – WiseLanka Knowledge Schema

## Version

0.1 Draft

## Purpose

This document defines the main entities, relationships and knowledge categories required by WiseLanka.

The schema is designed to support a comprehensive Sri Lankan education and career knowledge platform while allowing the initial demonstration to focus on a smaller set of well-developed learner scenarios.

WiseLanka will separate data from application logic so that institutions, programmes, qualifications, careers, skills, scholarships and policies can be expanded without redesigning the complete system.

## Core Knowledge Domains

WiseLanka organises its knowledge into the following major domains:

1. Learner
2. Education
3. Institution
4. Programme
5. Qualification
6. Career
7. Skill
8. Recognition and Policy
9. Scholarship and Opportunity
10. Pathway and Progression
11. Geography
12. Evidence and Verification
13. Recommendation and Explainability

## Core Entities

### 1. Learner

Represents a person using WiseLanka to explore education, skills and career pathways.

Possible information includes:

- Current qualification
- Subjects studied
- Grades
- Interests
- Career goals
- Preferred study mode
- Preferred location
- Budget
- Language preference
- Existing skills
- Saved pathways

### 2. Institution

Represents an organisation that provides education, training, certification or professional recognition.

Examples include:

- Government university
- Non-state higher education institution
- Technical college
- Vocational training institution
- Professional body
- International education provider
- Online learning provider

### 3. Programme

Represents a structured course of study offered by an institution.

Examples include:

- Certificate
- Foundation programme
- Diploma
- Higher diploma
- Degree
- Postgraduate diploma
- Master's degree
- Doctoral programme
- NVQ programme
- Professional programme

### 4. Qualification

Represents an officially awarded educational, vocational or professional achievement.

Examples include:

- GCE O/L
- GCE A/L
- Cambridge O Level
- Pearson Edexcel International A Level
- NVQ qualification
- Diploma
- Bachelor's degree
- Master's degree
- Professional certification

### 5. Career

Represents an occupation or professional destination that a learner may pursue.

Examples include:

- Software engineer
- Data scientist
- Accountant
- Teacher
- Nurse
- Quantity surveyor
- Graphic designer
- Entrepreneur

### 6. Skill

Represents an ability or competency required for education, employment or career progression.

Examples include:

- Python programming
- Communication
- Critical thinking
- English language
- Leadership
- Data analysis
- Project management
